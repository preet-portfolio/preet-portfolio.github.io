## What this says

- **Questions with your name in them go well; questions with only the app's name don't.**
  "Preet Panchal" pulls the right apps on both engines. "Deflate", "GeoLens", "DocFort" and
  "Agitate" alone are lost to the Mac Deflate, XLRI's GeoLens, an enterprise DocFort and a
  film-timer Agitate. This is the gap the structured data and the plain summaries are for.
- **Google barely surfaces the site.** It cites it on one question; it answers from the App
  Store listing instead. Search Console verification and the sitemap are the first fix there.
- **Bing has a stale copy.** It quotes "verified identity" for GeoLens, which the site no
  longer says. Submitting the sitemap through IndexNow after deploy should refresh it.
- **One answer is wrong about privacy:** Bing says Agitate collects data, from an unrelated
  Agitate. The new Agitate summary says, in one sentence, that it collects none.
